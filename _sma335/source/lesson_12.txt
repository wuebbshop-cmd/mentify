LESSON 12
  Power series solutions of First and Second order Equations

12.1 Introduction
You have learnt from the previous lesson that power series solutions need not exist for all
the differential equations and the nature of power series is different at ordinary points and
singular points of the equation.

In this lesson we shall find the power series solutions for the First and Second order
equations if the conditions for the existence of power series solutions are satisfied.

12.2 Objectives of the lesson
By the end of this lesson you should be able to:
   •  obtain the power series solutions of first and second order equations with variable
        coefficients when the conditions for the existence of power series solution are
        satisfied.
