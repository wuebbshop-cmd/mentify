Lesson 13
            Application of Differential Equations
13.1 Introduction
Differential Equations originate from the mathematical formulation of great variety of
problems in science and engineering.  In this lesson we consider problems that give rise
to some of the types of first and second order ordinary differential equations and the
system of equations studied in the previous lessons.  First we formulate the problems
mathematically and obtain a differential equation and then we solve the equation and
interpret the solutions.

13.2 Objective of the lesson
By the end of this lesson you will be able to:
•  formulate the differential equations from the given physical statements.
•  solve the  first and second order differential equations and system of equations
   formed.
