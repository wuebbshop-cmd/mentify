Welcome to Mentify Prep — Quick Start Guide

Scroll through these 5 quick steps to find your notes, past papers, and AI tutors.

1

Open Your Course

From your Dashboard, click any course card 1 under My Courses to open its syllabus, notes, and past papers.

2

Download Course Notes & PDF Pack

Click Download PDF Pack 1 to download all course PDFs as a ZIP, or select an individual lecture note / past paper 2 from the dropdown.

3

Access Topic Notes & Past Questions

Inside any unit, click Topic Notes & Explanations 1 to read structured notes, or switch to Authentic Past Questions 2 for real exam questions and worked solutions.

4

Ask the Topic AI Tutor

Click Ask a Topic Tutor 1 whenever you get stuck on a concept in that unit. You can also highlight any sentence in the notes to ask the AI Tutor directly.

5

Access MentifyAI (Snap & Solve Any Question)

Click MentifyAI 1 in the left menu anytime, then type a question or attach a photo of a homework problem 2 for step-by-step explanations.

You can reopen this anytime via Quick Guide in the menu.
