Privacy Policy

Your privacy is fundamental to us. This policy outlines how Mentify and Mentify Prep collect, use, and safeguard your personal data across our learning and exam revision platform.

Last updated: September 2026 • Applies to: All Learners, Students, Tutors, and Guardians

1 Information We Collect

We collect essential information required to deliver a personalized, secure, and effective study and revision experience:

  • Account Data: Full name, email address, phone number, user role, and profile preferences.
  • Course & Academic Progress: Course enrollments, syllabus study progress, completed practice variants, and exported study packs.
  • Uploaded Study Documents: Lecture notes, course outlines, and past exam sheets uploaded for syllabus mapping and text extraction.
  • Financial & Wallet Details: Mentify Prep credit transactions, subscription plan status, and payment references processed securely via Paystack / M-Pesa. We never store raw payment PINs or card details.

2 How We Use Your Information

Your data enables us to operate, manage, and continuously improve platform services:

  • Organizing and indexing course syllabus topics, lecture notes, and verified past questions.
  • Generating tailored practice question variants and step-by-step mathematical proofs.
  • Compiling and formatting complete PDF and Word study packs for your personal revision.
  • Tracking credit consumption balances and sending automated low-credit or subscription alerts.
  • Sending important account notifications, security alerts, and payment receipts.

3 Data Protection & AI Privacy Guarantees

Mentify employs industry-standard encryption, secure authentication tokens, and strict role-based access control. Your uploaded study documents and personalized derivations are processed strictly to fulfill your study requests and are never sold, rented, or used for public AI model training.

4 Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell personal data to advertisers. Data is processed only through trusted infrastructure and payment partners (such as Paystack for M-Pesa processing and secure server environments) strictly to deliver platform services.

5 Your Rights & Contact Information

You have the right to inspect, update, or request deletion of your personal data and uploaded materials at any time via your profile settings or by reaching out to our team.

If you have any questions or data privacy concerns, please reach out via our Contact Form or contact support.
